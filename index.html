Hydro Jetting FAQ

What is hydro jetting?

Hydro jetting uses a high-pressure stream of water, delivered through a specialized nozzle, to scour the inside walls of your drain and sewer lines. Unlike a cable, it removes grease, scale, sludge and roots instead of just poking a hole through them.

Is hydro jetting safe for my pipes?

On sound pipe, yes. That's why we camera-inspect every line before we jet. If the camera shows a collapsed, offset or badly deteriorated section, we'll tell you and recommend the right repair instead of risking more damage.

How is hydro jetting different from snaking?

A drain snake punches a hole through a clog. Hydro jetting cleans the full diameter of the pipe, so the buildup that caused the clog is gone — not waiting to close back over.

How long does it take?

Most residential main lines take one to three hours, including the before-and-after camera inspection.

Do you offer emergency hydro jetting?

Yes. We answer the phone 24 hours a day, 7 days a week at 404-201-3375, including nights, weekends and holidays.

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. We give you upfront pricing after the camera inspection and get your approval before any work begins. No surprise fees.
